A FEW THINGS TO KNOW

Game controls

Move with A/D and climb with W/S. Press Space to jump, Shift to run, and click to attack. Use E to interact and advance dialogue. F or T shifts time where the scene allows it. Escape opens the in-game pause menu.

On touch screens, use the left stick to move and climb. Push it all the way to run. The right buttons handle jump, claw, use, and time.

Music starts with the game. Use the sound button to mute it. Your browser must support WebGL 2.
