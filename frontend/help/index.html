Help

For order help, email support@unlim8ted.com. Include the product name and order or quote reference. Please do not send card details or passwords.

Do I need an account to browse?

No. You can browse projects, listen to free tracks, and view product pages without signing in. An account lets you keep a cart across sessions and use account features.

How much does shipping cost?

Select an available variant and enter your shipping address at checkout. You will see shipping, tax, and the total before paying. Physical items are made to order; production and delivery time depend on the item and destination.

What if my quote expires?

Shipping quotes last ten minutes. Calculate shipping again before paying. If you already submitted a payment, keep its reference and wait for confirmation. Do not start another checkout while a charge is being checked.

I was charged but my order is still processing.

Payment confirmation and fulfillment are separate steps. Keep your quote reference and email support if you need help. Do not pay for the same order again. The checkout service retries fulfillment when the supplier is temporarily unavailable.

Where is my digital download?

Free projects link to their reader, player, or repository. Paid downloads require a verified purchase. If your link is missing, contact support with your receipt reference; a cart entry is not proof of payment.

How do I reset my password?

Open Sign in and use the password recovery option. Check your spam folder for the reset email. If a session expires, sign in again.

Are all game and software features finished?

No. Read the status and requirements on the project page. Prototypes may be incomplete, and planned features may not be playable. For repository tools, check the README before installing.

For other questions, use the contact page.
